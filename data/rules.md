# Community 1 — London Agent Builders (fictional)

A London community for people who **build** AI agents. ~1,000 members (fictional), WhatsApp + Discord, monthly demo night.

## Membership criteria (used by the scorer)
1. **You build.** You are actively building with AI (agents, RAG, evals, tooling). Show it: a repo, demo, project, or a concrete description of what you're working on.
2. **Specifics over buzzwords.** Applications must say *what* you build or want to learn. Generic statements ("passionate AI enthusiast") without specifics → ask.
3. **London-based or regularly attending.** We meet in person in London. Remote-only applicants → ask.
4. **No pitching.** Sales, recruiting and investors are welcome as participants, but no cold DMs; job posts only in #jobs; product promo only in #show-and-tell with a demo. If your role is sales/recruiting/VC → ask to confirm.
5. **Zero tolerance (decline + report to shared blocklist):** crypto/forex "guaranteed returns", fake recruiters asking for ID/bank details, link spam / URL shorteners in the application, impersonating admins, phishing, copy-paste or bot-generated applications.

## Decisions
- **accept** – meets 1–3, no red flags.
- **ask** – plausible human but missing info, off-location, or rule-4 role. Send one follow-up question.
- **decline** – any rule-5 red flag, or blocklist match confirmed by another community.

## Appeals
Declined applicants can appeal via the form; a second organiser reviews within 7 days. Blocklist entries need evidence + reporter community and are hashed (phone, handle).

# Community 2 — South London Hack Nights (fictional)

A small organisers' circle (~150 fictional members) for people who **run** London tech meetups and hack nights. Purpose: share venues, speakers, and dates.

## Membership criteria
1. You organise or co-organise a tech community/event in London (say which one).
2. Screening question (required): "Which community do you run and roughly how many members?"
3. Promotion of paid products/courses only in the weekly promo thread.
4. Zero tolerance: same as London Agent Builders rule 5. **We subscribe to the shared blocklist** — a confirmed block in any partner community auto-flags the applicant here (human confirms).
