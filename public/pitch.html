The gap

Scammers hop. WhatsApp has no shared ban list.

  • Declined in community A → new handle in community B.
  • CAS, Mjolnir and FedBot are locked to one platform and only ask “known spammer?” — not “fits our rules?”
  • No verified shared blocklist for WhatsApp groups.
  • Volunteer leads re-read every application alone.

What we built · WhatsApp-first

Rules fit + shared hashes + human Send.

  • AI reviewer — accept / ask / decline with a plain-English reason citing the rule.
  • Shared blocklist — HMAC-SHA256 keyed hashes in Supabase, RLS per community, appeal path. No raw phone, email or handle.
  • WhatsApp Cloud API — signed webhook; apply in a 1:1 chat and get a reply.
  • Human in the loop — declines wait for a lead to click.
